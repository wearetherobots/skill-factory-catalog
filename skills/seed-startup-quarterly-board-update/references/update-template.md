# Board update layout

Use during Step 9. The first section must fit on one printed page. Omit a block only if it truly does not apply; if data is missing, keep the block with a `[MISSING: ...]` placeholder.

## Appendix

A. Monthly table: each month of the quarter plus the prior quarter's last month - cash, gross burn, net burn, core metric, customers/users, headcount.

B. Metric definitions and formulas used (copy from the definitions the founder confirmed). Note any definition changes from last quarter.

C. MRR bridge or cohort retention table, depending on business model.

D. Hiring: hires made, departures, open roles with target dates.

E. Product and customer detail, fundraising status (who has been contacted, stage, target close), follow-ups on last meeting's action items.

---

## Investor-update variant (optional)

If the founder also sends updates to non-board investors: keep headline, key metrics (founders often round or omit cash and runway here - their choice), wins, a short "what we're working on", and asks (usually intros and hiring referrals only). Remove detailed risks, board decisions and anything about personnel issues. Suggest the founder check with counsel on what is appropriate to share outside the board.
