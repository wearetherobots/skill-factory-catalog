# Platform rules and limits (Airbnb, Vrbo)

Platforms change fields and limits often. Treat every number below as "last known, verify current" and tell the manager when a limit could not be confirmed. Never quote a policy article number or URL from memory.

## Airbnb
- **Title**: historically capped at 50 characters. Verify current limit in the listing editor.
- **Description**: a main description plus optional sections such as The space, Guest access, Other details to note. Verify current per-section limits; write the hook so it works if only the first few lines show before "Show more".
- **Category ratings** guests leave: Cleanliness, Accuracy, Check-in, Communication, Location, Value. Listing copy directly drives Accuracy and Value.
- **Safety & property / disclosures**: Airbnb asks hosts to disclose items such as exterior security cameras, noise decibel monitors, pools/hot tubs without a gate or lock, nearby water, heights without rails, climbing/play structures, must climb stairs, potential for noise, pets living on property, no parking on property, shared spaces, amenity limitations, weapons on property, carbon monoxide and smoke alarm status. Verify the current list in the editor.
- **Cameras**: Airbnb's policy (as updated in 2024) prohibits security cameras inside listings regardless of location, requires disclosure of exterior cameras and noise decibel monitors, and limits where they may point. Treat any indoor camera as a Blocker and verify the current policy wording.
- **Parties**: Airbnb maintains a global party/event ban; do not write copy implying events are allowed ("perfect for parties", "event space"). Some event-host exceptions have existed historically; verify before advising otherwise.
- **Assistance animals**: Airbnb's accessibility/nondiscrimination policies require hosts to accommodate assistance animals even in no-pet listings, without pet fees. Verify current wording.
- **Nondiscrimination**: Airbnb has its own nondiscrimination policy that may be broader than federal law. See `fair-housing-language.md`.
- **Accessibility features**: Airbnb requires photos to support listed accessibility features (step-free entry, wide doorways, grab bars). Do not tick an accessibility feature without a supporting photo.
- **Review responses**: hosts can publicly respond to reviews for a limited window after posting. Verify the current window and length limit.
- **Off-platform contact**: do not put phone numbers, emails, or external URLs in listing text.

## Vrbo
- **Headline**: separate field from the property name; verify current character min/max.
- **Property description**: verify current minimum and maximum lengths; Vrbo listings often show a summary first, so front-load facts.
- **House rules**: Vrbo has structured fields (check-in/out, min age, max guests, pets, events, smoking); copy must match them.
- **Surveillance devices**: Vrbo requires disclosure of surveillance and recording devices and restricts their placement. Verify current policy; apply the stricter of Airbnb/Vrbo when listing on both.
- **Review responses**: owners can respond publicly; verify current timing and length rules.
- **Premier Host** status depends on reviews and booking behavior; accuracy complaints hurt it.

## Cross-listing rules
- Same facts on both platforms; wording may differ. Mismatched occupancy, bed counts, or pet rules across channels is a Major finding.
- Use the stricter platform's rule in shared copy (e.g., camera language).
- If a channel manager syncs content, warn that edits may be overwritten and to update the source record (manager's action, out of scope for you).

## Local requirements
Some US cities and counties require a registration or permit number, occupancy limit, or local contact to appear on listings. Do not determine whether a requirement applies. If the manager supplies a number or required text, place it in the platform's designated license/registration field or the description as instructed, and recommend they verify current local requirements.
