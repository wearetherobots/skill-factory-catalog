# Fair Housing and nondiscrimination language

How the federal Fair Housing Act applies to short-term rentals is not uniform and can depend on facts and jurisdiction; state and local laws may add protected classes; and Airbnb and Vrbo enforce their own nondiscrimination policies that can be stricter. Do not give a legal opinion. Apply the rule: describe the property and the conduct expected, never the kind of person who should or should not stay.

Federal protected classes under the Fair Housing Act: race, color, religion, sex (including sexual orientation and gender identity per current HUD interpretation; verify current status), national origin, familial status, disability. Platforms and states may add others (e.g., age, marital status, source of income); verify.

## Flag and rewrite
| Risky wording | Why | Safer alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Perfect for couples / singles / young professionals" | Steers by familial or marital status/age | "1 queen bed, ideal for 2 guests" |
| "No kids", "Adults only" in marketing copy | Familial status; platform policy | State hazards factually (pool ungated, balcony without rails, fragile decor) and use the platform's child-suitability setting; manager decides |
| "Family neighborhood", "quiet, mature area" | Implies who lives/stays there | "Residential street, low traffic" |
| "Safe neighborhood", "good area", "exclusive" | Can code for demographics | Concrete amenities: "well-lit parking", "gated entry" |
| "Near churches", "Christian home" | Religious preference | Name landmarks neutrally only if useful for wayfinding |
| "English-speaking guests", "Americans only" | National origin | Remove |
| "No wheelchairs", "Not for disabled guests" | Disability | Describe access facts: "3 steps to entry, no elevator, narrow 28-inch bathroom door" |
| "No service dogs", "Pets incl. ESAs $100" | Assistance animal policies | "Pets: up to 2 dogs, $X fee. Assistance animals welcome; no fee." (verify current platform policy) |
| "Must be married to share a room" | Marital status/sex | Remove |
| "Ideal for able-bodied guests" | Disability | Factual stair/access disclosure |

## Generally acceptable
Factual property and conduct statements: "Max 6 guests", "No parties or events", "Quiet hours 10 pm-8 am", "Unfenced pool", "Steep stairs", "Walking distance to the pier (0.3 mi)", "Family-friendly amenities: crib and high chair available" (describes amenities, does not exclude).

## Escalate to the manager
- A guest review or message alleging discrimination: do not draft a substantive public rebuttal; see `review-replies.md`.
- Owner instructions that require exclusionary wording: explain the risk, offer a factual alternative, and let the manager decide.
